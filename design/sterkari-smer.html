A

Vápno / lime

Like a well-made workshop manual. Chalky lime white, a wide confident grotesque, and one pigment blue, the way limewash is tinted. Clear, precise and calm.

  • Feels: precise, trustworthy, modern
  • Best for: marmorino and smooth finishes
  • Risk: can feel cool without warm photos

B

Beton / concrete

Building-site directness. Tall condensed headlines like formwork stencils, concrete-grey surfaces, and rust as the only warm colour. Says "you can do this" with some muscle.

  • Feels: bold, hands-on, unpretentious
  • Best for: concrete-look finishes, a younger audience
  • Risk: less inviting for home decorators

C

Hlína / clay

Calm and tactile, for people making a home. A soft stone background, a light literary serif, and clay and olive taken from natural plasters. Slower, warmer, more interior-magazine.

  • Feels: warm, crafted, relaxed
  • Best for: clay, lime and tadelakt finishes
  • Risk: less "you can do it" energy
